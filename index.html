Our Story

Our Story

Plant tissue culture is the foundation of modern plant science. But its underlying model has remained largely unchanged since the 1960s — and even at the scale it operates today, it depends on skilled people performing repetitive manual work across millions of plants.

NXT Bioscience is redesigning that system. By combining innovative plant science with robotics and automation, we are scaling tissue culture to meet global demand for clonal propagation, new genetics, and resilient supply chains.

Our Platform

Our Platform

A process built on human labour and outdated science cannot be scaled. Scaling tissue culture is not a robotics problem — it is a system-design problem.

NXT’s platform combines innovative plant science, redesigned from first principles, with robotics and automation built around it. Together, they redesign tissue culture as a propagation system for global scale.
