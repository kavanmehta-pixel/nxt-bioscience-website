Let’s talk

The platform expanding high-health clonal propagation at global scale

NXT brings innovative plant science, robotics, and automation together as a single system — built to scale tissue culture into the high-health propagation pipelines global agriculture, horticulture, and industry depend on.

Plant science, redesigned for scale

Plant tissue culture has worked the same way for decades. The science is well understood, but the systems around it were built for laboratory craft, not for the volumes propagation now requires.

NXT has done the work to bridge that gap. By redesigning the biology to work with automation — rather than asking automation to keep up with the biology — we have built a platform that scales tissue culture using methods operators will recognise.

Robotics and automation, purpose-built

Most automation attempts in tissue culture try to retrofit robots onto manual processes. The result is faster manual work, not scalable production.

NXT’s robotics are designed around the redesigned biology. Instead of moving one plant at a time, the hardware handles many simultaneously — turning a craft process into a production system.

One system, not separate parts

Plant science alone has been advancing for sixty years. Robotics alone is just hardware. The unlock is the combination — biology and machines designed to work as one propagation system.

NXT’s platform is protected by a global patent portfolio covering the integrated system, not a single invention.
