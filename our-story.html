Redesigning plant tissue culture for global scale

Our Story

NXT Bioscience exists to scale plant tissue culture beyond the limits of manual labour — combining innovative plant science with robotics and automation to meet global demand for clonal propagation, new genetics, and resilient supply chains.

A foundation worth building on

Plant tissue culture is the foundation of modern plant science. It has supported clean genetic deployment, varietal development, and clonal propagation at significant commercial scale for decades.

But the underlying model has remained largely unchanged since the 1960s. Even at the scale it operates today, tissue culture depends on skilled people performing repetitive manual work across millions of plants — and that model has reached the limit of what it can do.

The science is sound. The skill of the people running it is significant. The constraint is the system around them.

What changes when labour stops being the constraint

The economics of tissue culture have always been governed by labour. The cost of skilled hands moving plants one at a time has restricted TC to crops where the value per plant justifies the cost.

When labour stops being the constraint, the equation changes.

Crops that were never economically viable come within reach. Production moves closer to where plants are needed, not where labour is cheapest. Biosecurity strengthens. Supply chains shorten. Tissue culture stops being a specialist tool and becomes a credible answer to global propagation demand.

Where NXT fits

NXT Bioscience is redesigning plant tissue culture for global scale. By combining innovative plant science with robotics and automation, the platform removes labour as the cost barrier and brings tissue culture into the propagation pipelines global agriculture, horticulture, and industry depend on.

The work is grounded in serious science. Plant biology, media, and morphology have been redeveloped from first principles to work with automation rather than against it. The platform is protected by a global patent portfolio covering the integrated system, not a single invention.

NXT is Australian-developed technology, headquartered in Melbourne and built for a global market. We operate a world-class plant tissue culture R&D facility with a world-leading team.

What comes next

NXT’s platform is in active development across more than 30 crops, with new categories moving through proof-of-concept as it deploys globally.

For breeders, propagators, industry partners, and investors looking to remove labour as the cost barrier in high-health propagation supply chains, the conversation is open.
